    Claude Architect Workshop

    One story, Lumen Supply, an online electronics shop. Each lesson is a claim you test by running the agent: watch it fail, fix it, then prove the fix.

    Start readingPDFEPUB

    How each lesson runs

    • I do: Watch the failure and its fix.
    • We do: Change one setting at a time and predict.
    • You do: Fix the starting code, then test yourself.
    • Check: exam-style questions, each with why every option is right or wrong.
    • Play: one short drill on a mistake the quiz does not cover.
    • References: the primary sources, each with a line on why to read it.
